Research
======
{: #research}

**UAV 3D Coverage Path Planning Research**

Research Project, 2025.12 - Present

This project studies 3-cover coverage path planning on discrete 3D viewpoint graphs, jointly optimizing viewpoint selection, camera orientation, and closed-loop routes. I developed a PPO-guided tensorized GA planner with greedy seeding, tabu-style local search, and an interpretable symbolic scoring module for evaluating candidate plans.
