This machine-learning course project explores knowledge-graph augmented language modeling on a small LOTR corpus. I served as team leader for Task 1, covering data processing, model design, and training.

The pipeline includes text cleaning, paragraph segmentation, BPE tokenizer training, and 512-token autoregressive dataset construction. The model combines GCN-based knowledge-graph embeddings with a Transformer decoder and dense mixture-of-experts components through cross-attention.

The training process uses cross-entropy warm-up followed by lightweight reinforcement-learning reward shaping.
